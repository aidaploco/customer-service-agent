# Brightloop — Uptime and SLA

## Platform Uptime

Brightloop targets 99.9% monthly uptime for Starter, Pro, and Business plans, and 99.99% for Enterprise.

Historical uptime and incident history are published at status.brightloop.io.

## What Counts as Downtime

Downtime is defined as any period where the Brightloop API is unavailable or event delivery success rate falls below 95% for more than 5 consecutive minutes. Planned maintenance windows (announced at least 48 hours in advance) are excluded.

## Credits

If monthly uptime falls below the SLA threshold, customers are eligible for service credits:

| Uptime | Credit |
|--------|--------|
| 99.0% – 99.9% | 10% of monthly bill |
| 95.0% – 99.0% | 25% of monthly bill |
| Below 95.0%   | 50% of monthly bill |

Credits must be requested within 30 days of the incident. They apply to future invoices and are not redeemable for cash.

## Support Response Times

| Plan | Channel | Response SLA |
|------|---------|--------------|
| Starter | Email | 48 business hours |
| Pro | Email + Chat | 8 business hours |
| Business | Email + Chat | 4 business hours |
| Enterprise | Dedicated chat + Email | 1 business hour (P1 incidents) |

## Incident Communication

Brightloop communicates incidents via the status page (status.brightloop.io) and email alerts for customers who subscribe. Enterprise customers also receive direct chat notifications from their Customer Success Manager.
