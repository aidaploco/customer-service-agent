# Brightloop — Company Overview

Brightloop was founded in 2021 and is headquartered in San Francisco, CA. The company was started by a team of API infrastructure engineers who wanted to make webhook delivery and event-driven integrations reliable for every engineering team, regardless of scale.

Brightloop's mission is to make real-time data pipelines simple, observable, and fault-tolerant. We believe developers should spend time building products, not debugging dropped webhooks or retrying failed API calls.

The company is privately held and backed by leading infrastructure-focused venture funds.

## Leadership

Brightloop is led by a small executive team with deep roots in developer tooling and distributed systems. The founding team previously built infrastructure at large-scale API and developer-platform companies.

## Culture and Values

Brightloop operates as a fully remote company with team members across North America and Europe. We hold a developer-first mindset: our own engineers are our first customers, and every product decision goes through a "would we use this ourselves?" filter.

Core values: transparency, reliability, and simplicity over cleverness.
