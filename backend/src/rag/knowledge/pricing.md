# Brightloop — Plans and Pricing

Brightloop offers three tiers. All plans include the core webhook delivery engine, dashboard access, and HMAC-SHA256 signing.

## Starter — $49/month

For indie developers and small projects.

- Up to 500,000 events/month
- 3 endpoints
- 7-day event history and replay
- Standard retry policy (5 attempts, exponential backoff)
- Email support (48-hour response SLA)
- 1 team member seat

## Pro — $199/month

For growing teams with production workloads.

- Up to 5,000,000 events/month
- Unlimited endpoints
- 30-day event history and replay
- Custom retry policies per endpoint
- Payload transformations
- Priority email + chat support (8-hour response SLA)
- 10 team member seats
- Webhook signature rotation

## Enterprise — Custom pricing

For companies with compliance requirements, high volume, or dedicated support needs.

- Unlimited events
- Unlimited endpoints and team members
- 90-day event history
- Dedicated infrastructure options (single-tenant)
- SSO / SAML support
- SOC 2 Type II audit reports on request
- 99.99% uptime SLA
- Dedicated Slack channel with your Customer Success Manager
- Custom data retention and export

Contact sales@brightloop.io for Enterprise pricing.

## Free Trial

All plans include a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. Trial accounts are limited to 10,000 events.

## Annual Billing

Annual plans receive a 20% discount compared to monthly billing.
